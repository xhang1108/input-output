# FAQ & Troubleshooting

## Why is my connection red?
Red connections indicate a type mismatch. For example, you cannot connect a matrix output to a port that only accepts numeric input.

## How do I reset the view?
Double-click on an empty space on the canvas or press the Spacebar.

## How do I export my charts?
Currently, you can use the IOGL protocol for exporting and importing.
